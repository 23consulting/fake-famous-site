Questions

How much does it cost?

Fake Famous Pro is €3.99 a month or €25.99 a year. You pay at checkout, get full access straight away, and the plan renews automatically until you cancel.

Is anything actually broadcast?

No. Fake Famous shows a simulated live screen on your own device. Nothing goes to any social media platform, and no real people are watching.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It runs in your browser on iPhone, Android and desktop. Add it to your home screen for a full-screen view.

Can I post the recording?

Recordings are yours to download. Use them for fun, skits and practice. If you share one, make clear it's a simulation and follow the rules of the platform you post on.

Can I use it to fake my followers or engagement?

No. Fake Famous is for pranks, comedy and practice. Don't use it to claim a real audience, follower count, views or earnings, for example to win sponsorships, brand deals or donations. That breaks our Terms and we'll close accounts that do it.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Cancel any time from the link in your receipt email. Changed your mind? You can get a full refund within 30 days of any payment at paddle.net. Payments are handled by Paddle, our merchant of record. Refund terms are in our refund policy.

Is Fake Famous connected to any social media app?

No. It's an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any social media platform. The live screens are original designs, not copies of any app. The layout names describe the style of live screen.
